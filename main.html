Frequently asked questions

How do I pair my WhatsApp device?

Choose Deploy, then select QR Code or Pairing Code. Finish linking in WhatsApp under Settings → Linked Devices.

Do I need to include my country code?

Yes. Include your country code when requesting a pairing code. You can enter spaces or punctuation; they are removed automatically.

Should I share my QR code or pairing code?

No. Use the QR code or pairing code only in WhatsApp on your own device. Keep all pairing and session details private.

What if the QR code does not load?

Choose Deploy, select QR Code, and try generating a new code. QR codes are temporary, so scan one as soon as it appears.

When will Plugins be available?

Plugins are coming soon. The Plugins tile will show an update when the feature is ready.
